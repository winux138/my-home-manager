# General Preferences

- Keep changes small and reviewable.
- Do not add placeholders, speculative structure, or unused dependencies.
- Prefer strong types over raw strings and loose shapes.
- Avoid hidden state or surprising side effects.
- Use ASCII characters only everywhere in repository files. Avoid Unicode arrows, smart quotes,
  en/em dashes, ellipsis, box drawing, and emoji. Use `->`, `-`, `'`, `"`, and `...` instead.
